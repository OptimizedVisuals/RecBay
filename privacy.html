Privacy policy.

This privacy policy describes how RecBay handles information submitted through the contact form on this website.

Information we collect

What we collect and why

When you use the contact form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, the type of project you're inquiring about, budget range, timeline, and the project details you write in the message field.

This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and to coordinate potential production work. We do not sell or share your details with third parties, except where required by law or by the form-processing service used to deliver the form.

No analytics, cookies, or tracking are currently enabled on this site. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated and cookie consent will be requested before any non-essential tracking loads.

Form submissions

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, and forwarded to RecBay's business email. Inquiry submissions are kept for up to 12 months so we can follow up on ongoing conversations, and are deleted sooner on request. Submissions are not used for marketing unless you explicitly agree.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting RecBay at the business email listed on the contact page.
