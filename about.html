Why RecBay

We build films frame by frame.

RecBay started from the idea that a production team should be as deliberate about the work as it is about the result. There is no house style here — only the style each idea calls for.

We work across film, photography, and motion because the idea should choose the medium, not the other way around. Whether it's a commercial, a brand film, an editorial series, or a color grade, the question is always the same: what does this idea need to become?
